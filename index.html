Waxing Crescent Moon

"The Silver Katana"

The Waxing Crescent is the earliest sliver of the moon visible after the New Moon. It rises in the east shortly after the Sun and sets after dark in the west. This phase is ideal for spotting Earthshine — the pale glow on the unlit portion caused by sunlight bouncing off Earth. The crescent grows each day as the Moon moves away from the Sun.

💡 Lunar Illumination

22% Illuminated

Lunar illumination refers to the percentage of the Moon's surface visible from Earth that is lit by direct sunlight. At 22%, only a small sliver is bright — perfect for deep-sky observation since the Moon does not wash out faint objects. As illumination approaches 100% (Full Moon), light pollution from the Moon can reduce stargazing quality significantly.
